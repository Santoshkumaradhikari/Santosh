> **Hemisphere 1 — Qualitative Defence**
> Can you trust this company, and can you actually trade it?
> - Governance & Promoter Behaviour **15**
> - Liquidity & Tradability **10**
> - Sector & Business Model Durability **15**
> **40 points**
>
> **Hemisphere 2 — Quantitative Forensics**
> Setting trust aside, what do the numbers actually say?
> - Financial Strength & Profitability **20**
> - Valuation Reasonableness **15**
> - Growth Trajectory **15**
> - Dividend & Capital Return Discipline **10**
> **60 points**

> **Two angles, one score.** Hemisphere 1 screens out companies you can't trust or can't trade, regardless of how good the numbers look. Hemisphere 2 does the financial statement work. Chapters 63–66 combine both sides into a single Canon Score out of 100.

> **The rubric.** Seven dimensions, added together — never averaged — so the ceiling is always exactly 100. Financial Strength carries the most weight because a company that can't make safe money eventually fails at everything else; full rubric in Chapter 64.
