### Relevance to open items

- **EXH-001 / CLM-0010 (credit lead 6–18 months):** "Claims on Private Sector (y-o-y)" is the official series the book's ch-0-1 refers to ("NRB publishes monthly banking system statistics including private sector credit growth"). The annual series above covers only FY2022/23–FY2025/26; the test needs FY2020/21 onward (to include the ~25% credit peak and the 2021–22 correction) and ideally monthly — Table 70 "Monthly Situation of Major Economic Indicators" and the Annex time-series are the next fetch targets.
- **ch-0-1 regime table:** the book's "early expansion 15–20%" / "peak expansion 20–30%" / "tightening below 10%" bands: the four official years above (4.6 / 6.1 / 8.1 / 6.6) all sit in the book's "credit tightening / re-expansion" territory (below 10% / 10–15% recovering) — a useful sanity anchor once the longer monthly series is in.
- **CON-0007 (rally start):** Tables 63 (Stock Market Indicators) and 66 (Structure of Share Price Indices) in the same workbook contain NEPSE index history — pending fetch.
