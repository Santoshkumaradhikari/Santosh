Decision Rules

The 4 Canon Decision Tiers

A composite score maps directly into portfolio allocation limits, preventing emotional capital misallocation.

80 – 100 Points

Tier 1: Prime Compounder

Unimpeachable governance, high sustainable ROE, robust liquidity. Eligible for maximum conviction portfolio weighting.

65 – 79 Points

Tier 2: Tactical Quality

Solid business model with minor cyclical or valuation drags. Core secondary holding with active monitoring.

50 – 64 Points

Tier 3: Speculative Buffer

Acceptable financials but challenged governance or thin liquidity. Strict 3% to 5% portfolio position ceiling.

< 50 Points

Tier 4: Auto-Disqualification

Chronic equity dilution, negative operating cash flow, or compromised promoter integrity. Zero capital permitted.

Complete Rubric

The 100-Point Scoring Specification

Seven dimensions, summed additively — never averaged — ensuring that the ceiling is always exactly 100.

# Dimension Hemisphere Weight Diagnostic Focus
01 Financial Strength & Profitability Quantitative Forensics 20 pts Real cash conversion, loan-loss provisioning, interest coverage, sustainable ROE.
02 Governance & Promoter Behaviour Qualitative Defence 15 pts Promoter integrity, related-party transactions, share pledging, regulatory track record.
03 Valuation Reasonableness Quantitative Forensics 15 pts P/E, P/B, and EV/EBITDA anchored against the risk-free 364-day Treasury Bill rate.
04 Sector & Business Model Durability Qualitative Defence 15 pts Competitive moats, PPA tariff structures, regulatory barriers to entry.
05 Growth Trajectory Quantitative Forensics 15 pts 5-year EPS CAGR outperforming the company's paid-up capital dilution rate.
06 Liquidity & Tradability Qualitative Defence 10 pts Free float percentage, daily NEPSE turnover, order book depth, impact cost.
07 Dividend & Capital Return Discipline Quantitative Forensics 10 pts Cash dividend consistency, bonus shares backed by real free reserves.
