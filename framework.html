Decision Rules

The 4 Canon Decision Tiers

A composite score maps directly into portfolio allocation limits, preventing emotional capital misallocation.

80 – 100 Points

Tier 1: Prime Compounder

Unimpeachable governance, high sustainable ROE, robust liquidity. Eligible for maximum conviction portfolio weighting.

65 – 79 Points

Tier 2: Tactical Quality

Solid business model with minor cyclical or valuation drags. Core secondary holding with active monitoring.

50 – 64 Points

Tier 3: Speculative Buffer

Acceptable financials but challenged governance or thin liquidity. Strict 3% to 5% portfolio position ceiling.

< 50 Points

Tier 4: Auto-Disqualification

Chronic equity dilution, negative operating cash flow, or compromised promoter integrity. Zero capital permitted.
