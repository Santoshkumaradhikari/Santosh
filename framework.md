Every listed company on NEPSE is audited across **two distinct hemispheres**:

```
                       THE DUAL-HEMISPHERE DECISION ENGINE

  │  HEMISPHERE 1: QUALITATIVE DEFENCE (40 PTS)   │ HEMISPHERE 2: QUANTITATIVE (60 PTS│
  │  "Can you trust it, and can you trade it?"    │ "What do the audited numbers say?"│
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
```
