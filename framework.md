## 5. Master Scoring Rubric Specification

| # | Dimension | Hemisphere | Max Pts | Forensic Diagnostic Checklist |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **01** | **Financial Strength & Profitability** | Quantitative | **20** | Operating cash conversion, loan-loss provisioning, interest coverage, sustainable ROE. |
| **02** | **Governance & Promoter Behaviour** | Qualitative | **15** | Clean regulatory record, transparent related-party transactions, lock-in integrity. |
| **03** | **Valuation Reasonableness** | Quantitative | **15** | P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA anchored against the NRB 364-day Treasury Bill rate. |
| **04** | **Sector & Business Durability** | Qualitative | **15** | Competitive moats, PPA tariff safety, regulatory barriers to entry. |
| **05** | **Growth Trajectory** | Quantitative | **15** | 5-Year EPS CAGR outperforming the company's paid-up capital dilution rate. |
| **06** | **Liquidity & Tradability** | Qualitative | **10** | Public float %, daily NEPSE trading volume, order book depth, impact cost. |
| **07** | **Dividend & Capital Return** | Quantitative | **10** | Reliable cash dividend track record; bonus shares backed by real retained earnings. |
| **—** | **TOTAL CANON SCORE** | **Composite** | **100** | **Complete 100-Point Security Evaluation** |
