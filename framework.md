## 2. The 100-Point Canon Scoring Rubric

The Canon Score evaluates every listed company across **two distinct hemispheres**, totaling exactly 100 points.

```
                          THE 100-POINT CANON SCORING RUBRIC
   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┐
   │ HEMISPHERE 1: QUALITATIVE DEFENCE             │ HEMISPHERE 2: QUANTITATIVE FORENSICS│
   │ (Can you trust it, and can you trade it?)     │ (What do the audited numbers say?) │
   ├───────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
   │ 1. Governance & Promoter Behaviour   : 15 pts │ 4. Financial Strength & Profitability : 20 pts│
   │ 2. Liquidity & Tradability           : 10 pts │ 5. Valuation Reasonableness           : 15 pts│
   │ 3. Sector & Business Model Durability: 15 pts │ 6. Growth Trajectory                  : 15 pts│
   │                                               │ 7. Dividend & Capital Return          : 10 pts│
   ├───────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
   │ Subtotal: 40 Points                           │ Subtotal: 60 Points               │
   └───────────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘
                           TOTAL CANON SCORE = 100 POINTS
```

### Rubric Breakdown by Dimension

| # | Dimension | Weight | Primary Diagnostic Focus |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Financial Strength & Profitability** | **20 pts** | Real operating cash flow, NPL provisions, debt coverage, ROE sustainability. |
| **2** | **Governance & Promoter Behaviour** | **15 pts** | Promoter integrity, related-party transactions, lock-in compliance. |
| **3** | **Valuation Reasonableness** | **15 pts** | P/E, P/B, and EV/EBITDA vs. earnings power and risk-free anchor. |
| **4** | **Sector & Business Model Durability** | **15 pts** | Regulatory moats, PPA tariffs, competitive position. |
| **5** | **Growth Trajectory** | **15 pts** | Organic revenue and EPS CAGR vs. capital dilution rate. |
| **6** | **Liquidity & Tradability** | **10 pts** | Free float, daily turnover, impact cost, execution risk. |
| **7** | **Dividend & Capital Return** | **10 pts** | Cash dividend discipline, sustainable bonus payouts from free reserves. |
| **TOTAL** | **The Canon Rubric** | **100 pts** | **Universal 100-Point Composite Score** |
