## ❌ ERRORS FOUND (3)

### 1. Chapter 90 — wrong date for the all-time high
The book: NEPSE "closed at 3,198.60 points on **August 8, 2021**" (and "early August 2021").
Reality: the 3,198.60 all-time closing high was set on **August 18, 2021** (Bhadra 2, 2078). August 3 saw 3,111.09; the peak came two weeks later.
**Fix:** change "August 8" → "August 18" and "early August" → "mid-August".

### 2. Chapter 10 — pre-open session time is wrong
The book: "Pre-Open Session 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM NST."
Reality: NEPSE's pre-open order-collection window is ~**10:30 AM** onward (sources give 10:30–10:45 with matching before the 11:00 open) — not a full hour from 10:00.
**Fix:** state "approximately 10:30 AM–11:00 AM, with order entry closing before the open auction" and advise checking the current NEPSE circular.

### 3. Chapter 36 — deposit-interest withholding for companies
The book's table: bank/FI deposit interest paid to a **resident company** withheld at "5% (advance)".
Reality: the 5% concessional rate under ITA §88(3) applies to **individuals** only; interest paid to resident entities is generally withheld at **15%** under §88(1) (BFI-to-BFI interest is exempt).
**Fix:** change the entity row to 15% (with the BFI exemption note). The individual rows (5% final) are correct.

## ⚠️ IMPRECISE OR AGING (4)

1. **Ch 0.1 — "Banking stocks constitute approximately 60–70% of NEPSE market capitalisation."** True only if "banking" means the whole BFI + insurance complex, and closer to the historical picture. Commercial banks alone are well under half today, and hydropower's weight has grown sharply. Suggest: "the banking and financial complex (banks, development banks, finance, microfinance, insurance) historically made up roughly half to two-thirds of market cap."
2. **Ch 7 — "listed universe at approximately 230 companies."** Slightly stale: current counts run ~250 (≈284 including listed funds). Suggest "roughly 250" or date-stamp the figure.
3. **Ch 4 — import cover "above 22 months."** NRB's 8-month FY 2082/83 data: 21.4 months (merchandise) / 18.5 months (goods + services). The claim is near-true for merchandise-only at earlier points, but "above 22" overstates the standard measure. Suggest quoting the goods+services figure with its date.
4. **Ch 90 — 2022 trough described as "1,900–2,000 range by mid-2022."** The actual 2022 low was 1,807–1,848 (June 23, 2022). The later "high 1,800s by December" is right; the mid-2022 characterization is soft. Suggest adding the June 1,848 low explicitly.
