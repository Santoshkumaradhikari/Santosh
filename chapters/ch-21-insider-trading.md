> *"The retail investor who buys because 'the price is moving, someone must know something' is very often the counterparty of last resort to an insider selling into the rally."*

In NEPSE, where public floats are thin, promoter ownership is concentrated, and financial media coverage is sparse, the gap between when a corporate event is decided and when it is formally published is where **Insider Trading** operates.

---

## Lesson 21.1 — What Insider Trading Is, and Why Thin Markets Suffer It Most

Under **Section 91 of the Securities Act, 2063 (2006)**, insider trading (*भित्री कारोबार*) occurs when a person buys, sells, or causes others to transact based on **unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI)**.

```
                             WHY THIN MARKETS MAGNIFY INSIDER RISK
   ┌───────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Deep Institutional Exchanges      │ NEPSE Thin-Float Microstructure   │
   ├───────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
   │ • Institutional order flow dilutes│ • Low public float means small    │
   │   informed trades across deep book│   insider orders cause huge % jumps│
   │ • Continuous real-time filings.   │ • Periodic, batch announcements.  │
   │ • High automated surveillance.    │ • Retail flow easily manipulated. │
   └───────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Lesson 21.4 — Recognising the Signatures of Informed Trading

| Observable Market Signature | High-Probability Interpretation | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Announcement Volume Surge** | Information circulating informally prior to official circular. | Do not chase; wait for filing. |
| **Single-Broker Code Concentration** | 60%+ of buy volume originating from one specific broker desk. | High risk of informed pump. |
| **Exact Ratio Leaked on Socials** | Specific bonus/rights percentages appearing in Viber groups. | Avoid buying late-stage top. |
| **Post-Announcement Reversal Dump** | Stock crashes immediately after "positive" earnings published. | Classic "buy rumor, sell news". |

## Lesson 21.6 — The Macro Cost: A Tax on Market Maturation

Uncontrolled insider trading increases the cost of equity capital across the economy. Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) and long-term domestic funds demand a higher equity risk premium to compensate for informational disadvantage, suppressing overall NEPSE valuation multiples.

### Chapter Summary & Core Takeaways

- [ ] **Section 91 Prohibits Insider Trading:** Covers promoters, directors, auditors, and brokers.
- [ ] **Thin Floats Magnify Price Distortions:** Modest insider volume triggers outsized percentage spikes in tightly held scrips.
- [ ] **Discipline Beats Asymmetry:** Compete on patience and balance sheet forensics rather than trying to out-rumour insiders.
- [ ] **Position Sizing is Your Best Hedge:** Limit allocation to low-float stocks showing suspicious volume anomalies.
