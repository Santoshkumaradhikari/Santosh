> *"A governance score is not a substitute for financial analysis — it is a multiplier. A cheap stock with terrible governance is not a bargain; it is a trap waiting for the right catalyst."*

Instinct and market stories are not an investment system. Converting promoter behaviour, board independence, related-party dealings, and disclosure quality into a **structured, repeatable scoring rubric** provides a disciplined paper trail for portfolio construction.

## Lesson 23.2 — Design Principles for a Nepal-Buildable Scorecard

Built specifically for Nepal's public disclosure ecosystem (*Companies Act 2063, BAFIA 2073, SEBON CG Directives 2074*):

1. **Score Only What Is Disclosed:** Unstated data is recorded as an absence of transparency.
2. **Prioritize 3-to-5 Year Trends:** A promoter stake falling from 58% to 45% is more informative than a static snapshot.
3. **Reward Voluntary Transparency:** Credit companies that publish detailed related-party schedules beyond statutory minimums.
4. **Standardize Raw Inputs:** Completed entirely from Annual Reports, AGM Notices, and NEPSE filings.

## Lesson 23.3 — The Six-Dimension NEPSE Governance Scoring Rubric (0–12 Scale)

| Dimension | Diagnostic Focus | Score 0 (Poor) | Score 1 (Fair) | Score 2 (Strong) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Board Independence** | Independent vs. Promoter board seats. | No independent directors; family-dominated. | Bare statutory minimum met; limited criteria. | $\ge 2$ independent directors; diverse board. |
| **2. Related-Party Deals** | NFRS related-party footnote granularity. | Opaque/missing notes despite active affiliates. | Aggregated figures without counterparty names. | Itemized by counterparty, terms, & board review. |
| **3. Promoter Trends** | 3–5 year promoter shareholding trajectory. | Substantial unexplained OTC sell-downs. | Roughly stable with routine minor shifts. | Stable or rising; full rights subscription. |
| **4. Disclosure Quality** | Timeliness of AGMs and quarterly filings. | Chronic AGM delays; missing quarterly filings. | Minor past filing lapses; complete filings. | Consistently on-time AGMs & searchable reports. |
| **5. Capital Return History**| Dividend consistency & rights issue pricing. | Dilutive at-par rights; erratic payouts. | Unremarkable history; heavy stock dividends. | Consistent cash/bonus policy; fair rights terms. |
| **6. Audit & Tenure** | Auditor independence, rotation, & opinion. | Qualified opinion; decade-long unrotated firm. | Clean opinion; long unrotated tenure. | Clean opinion; regular rotation & audit oversight. |

### Governance Grade Bands (0–12 Total Points)

## Lesson 23.5 — Using the Score in Practice

### 1. Position Sizing Ceilings
- **Grade A & B:** Eligible for standard position sizing based on valuation.
- **Grade C:** Position capped at maximum **50% of normal sizing**.
- **Grade D:** **Zero capital allocation**.

### 2. Valuation Multiple Haircut
Apply a **15% to 30% discount** on P/B and P/E targets for Grade C companies to reflect the risk of rent extraction.

## Lesson 23.6 — Forensic Boundaries: What Public Scorecards Cannot Catch

```
                               THE LIMITS OF PUBLIC SCORING
   ┌───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┐
   │ Undisclosed Deals     │ Nominee Holdings      │ Backward-Looking Lag  │
   │ Transactions omitted  │ Shares held via       │ Scores reflect past   │
   │ entirely from NFRS    │ unlisted benami trusts│ fiscal year filings,  │
   │ footnotes.            │ or shell entities.    │ lagging live decay.   │
   └───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┘
```

> **Forensic Rule:** A score of 2 means *"no red flags found in public disclosures"*, not *"guaranteed absence of private misconduct"*. Always pair governance scoring with financial forensics.
