## Lesson 17.1 — What Is a Debenture: Debt Instrument Basics & Legal Architecture

A **debenture** is a formal long-term debt certificate governed by the *Companies Act, 2063* and *Securities Act, 2063*.

```
                                ANATOMY OF A NEPALI DEBENTURE
   ┌───────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Core Parameter        │ Nepali Market Standard                                    │
   ├───────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   │ Face Value (Par)      │ NPR 1,000 per unit (standard face value on NEPSE).        │
   │ Coupon Rate           │ Fixed annual coupon (typically 7.0% to 11.5%).            │
   │ Coupon Frequency      │ Semi-annual or annual interest payouts.                   │
   │ Maturity (Tenor)      │ 5 to 10 years (5–7 years for banks, 7–10 for hydro).      │
   │ Credit Rating         │ Mandated by SEBON (ICRA Nepal, CARE Nepal, Infomerics).   │
   │ Subordinated Tier-II  │ Commercial bank debentures qualify as Tier-II capital.   │
   └───────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Insolvency Waterfall under the *Insolvency Act, 2063*

In the event of corporate liquidation, claims are settled in strict statutory priority:
1. **Secured Creditors:** Backed by fixed charges over physical assets.
2. **Statutory & Labor Dues:** Government taxes and employee wages.
3. **Debenture Holders & Unsecured Creditors:** General floating charge or uncollateralized claims.
4. **Equity Shareholders:** Residual claimants (often completely wiped out in severe insolvency).

## Lesson 17.2 — Corporate Debentures Listed on NEPSE: Available Instruments

NEPSE hosts listed corporate debentures across two primary sectors:

1. **Commercial Banks:** Issued primarily to satisfy NRB Basel-III supplementary Tier-II capital requirements and fund long-tenor loan books.
2. **Hydropower & Energy Issuers:** Long-dated debt matching predictable, contracted PPA tariff cash flows once operational.

## Lesson 17.3 — Government Securities: PDMO & NRB Auction Process

Under the **Public Debt Act, 2079 (सार्वजनिक ऋण व्यवस्थापन ऐन, २०७९)**, domestic sovereign borrowing is planned by the **Public Debt Management Office (PDMO)** under the Ministry of Finance, with **Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB)** operating the competitive auction platform.

```
                           GOVERNMENT DEBT INSTRUMENTS IN NEPAL
   ┌───────────────────────┬────────────────┬──────────────────────────┬──────────────┐
   │ Instrument            │ Maturity       │ Return Structure         │ Target Bidders│
   ├───────────────────────┼────────────────┼──────────────────────────┼──────────────┤
   │ Treasury Bills        │ 28 to 364 Days │ Discount to Face Value   │ BFIs & Inst. │
   │ Development Bonds     │ 2 to 15 Years  │ Semi-Annual Fixed Coupon │ BFIs, Funds  │
   │ Citizen Savings Bonds │ 3 to 5 Years   │ Fixed High-Yield Coupon  │ Retail Public│
   │ Remittance Bonds      │ 5 Years        │ Preferential Fixed Yield │ Migrant Labor│
   └───────────────────────┴────────────────┴──────────────────────────┴──────────────┘
```

## Lesson 17.4 — Yield, Coupon, and Price Relationship: The Inverse Rule & Duration

### The Fundamental Mathematical Identity

$$\text{Bond Price} = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{\text{Coupon}}{(1 + y)^t} + \frac{\text{Face Value}}{(1 + y)^n}$$

When market yields rise, fixed coupon cash flows are discounted at a higher rate, forcing bond prices below par (discount). When market yields drop, prices rise above par (premium).

### Worked Example: 5-Year, 8% Coupon Debenture (Par Rs. 1,000)

$$\text{Price at 10\% Yield} = \frac{80}{1.10^1} + \frac{80}{1.10^2} + \frac{80}{1.10^3} + \frac{80}{1.10^4} + \frac{1080}{1.10^5} = \mathbf{\text{NPR 924.07}}$$

$$\text{Price at 6\% Yield} = \frac{80}{1.06^1} + \frac{80}{1.06^2} + \frac{80}{1.06^3} + \frac{80}{1.06^4} + \frac{1080}{1.06^5} = \mathbf{\text{NPR 1,084.25}}$$

```
                          PRICE-YIELD INVERSE SENSITIVITY CURVE
          Bond Price
             ▲
             │       * Premium (Price = NPR 1,084.25 @ 6% Yield)
             │        \
   NPR 1,000 ┼─────────* Par (Price = NPR 1,000 @ 8% Yield)
             │            \
             │             * Discount (Price = NPR 924.07 @ 10% Yield)
             └────────────────────────────────────────────────► Market Yield
```

## Lesson 17.5 — Default Risk, Trustee Fiduciary Duty, and Credit Surveillance

### Credit Risk Surveillance

SEBON requires corporate debt issuers to maintain continuous ratings by licensed rating agencies:
- **ICRA Nepal**
- **CARE Ratings Nepal**
- **Infomerics Credit Rating Nepal**

### Taxation of Debentures in Nepal

Under the *Income Tax Act, 2058*, interest income earned from corporate debentures and government citizen savings bonds by resident individual investors is subject to a **5% Final Withholding Tax (WHT)** deducted at source.

## Lesson 17.6 — How Bond Yields Anchor Equity Valuations: The Gravitational Constant

The yield on the **364-day Treasury Bill** acts as the baseline **Risk-Free Rate ($R_f$)** for the Nepali economy.

### The Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) Transmission

$$\text{Cost of Equity } (K_e) = R_f + \beta \times \text{ERP}$$

```
                           INTEREST RATE TRANSMISSION TO NEPSE
   ┌───────────────────────────────────┐       ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
   │ NRB Tightens Monetary Policy      │       │ NRB Eases Monetary Policy         │
   │ T-Bill Yields Rise (e.g. 10%–12%) │       │ T-Bill Yields Fall (e.g. 3%–5%)   │
   ├───────────────────────────────────┤       ├───────────────────────────────────┤
   │ • Discount rate ($K_e$) spikes.   │       │ • Discount rate ($K_e$) drops.    │
   │ • Fixed deposits attract capital. │       │ • Equity dividend yields appeal.  │
   │ • NEPSE P/E multiples compress.   │       │ • NEPSE equity multiples expand.  │
   └───────────────────────────────────┘       └───────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Chapter Summary & Core Takeaways

- [ ] **Price and Yield Move Inversely:** Rising rates cause mark-to-market bond losses; falling rates produce capital gains.
- [ ] **T-Bills Anchor Everything:** NRB Treasury Bill yields set the baseline discount hurdle for all equity investments.
- [ ] **Seniority Safeguard:** Debenture holders enjoy statutory priority over equity shareholders during corporate liquidations.
- [ ] **5% Final WHT:** Interest on debentures is taxed at a flat 5% final withholding rate for retail individuals.
