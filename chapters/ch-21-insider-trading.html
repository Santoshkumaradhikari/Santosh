The retail investor who buys because 'the price is moving, someone must know something' is very often the counterparty of last resort to an insider selling into the rally.

Lesson 21.1 — What Insider Trading Is

Under Section 91 of the Securities Act, 2063, trading on unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI) is illegal. In NEPSE, thin public floats magnify the price impact of insider buying or selling relative to deep international markets.

Lesson 21.2 — Nepal's Legal Architecture

The original Securities Act 2063 prescribed maximum 1-year imprisonment. The Securities (First Amendment) Bill proposes fines up to NPR 30 Million and 3-year prison terms with expanded authority to audit bank transactions. High-profile domestic cases include Ridi Power and the Sarbottam Cement book-building controversy.

Lesson 21.3 — Information Transmission Channels

Information flows from promoter boardrooms to broker dealing desks, before leaking into retail social media groups (Viber, Telegram, Facebook) to trigger retail FOMO buying.

Lesson 21.4 — Signatures of Informed Trading

Market Signature High-Probability Meaning Defensive Action
Pre-Announcement Volume Spike Information circulating ahead of official filing Wait for formal regulatory disclosure
Single-Broker Concentration Coordinated buying via one broker desk Avoid chasing speculative pumps
Specific Bonus Rumours on Socials Information leaked through intermediary chain Never buy at the tail-end of rumour moves
Post-Announcement Dump Informed sellers taking profits into the news Strictly avoid buying earnings day pops

Lesson 21.5 — Retail Defensive Rules

  • Trade exclusively on disclosed, filed corporate announcements.
  • Cap position sizes in thinly floated, promoter-concentrated scrips.
  • Track mandatory 30-day lock-in expiry announcements to anticipate supply overhangs.

Lesson 21.6 — The Macro Economic Cost

Persistent informational asymmetry deters long-term institutional capital, increases retail volatility, and elevates the required equity risk premium across all NEPSE-listed businesses.
