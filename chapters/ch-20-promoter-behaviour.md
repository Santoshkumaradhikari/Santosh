> *"Related-party transactions, promoter share pledging, and boardroom capture explain more of the permanent capital loss suffered by NEPSE investors than any P/E multiple or technical breakout ever will."*

In NEPSE, financial statement ratios only reveal what happens after management and the board make decisions. In a concentrated market where founding families and business houses control 51% to 70% of equity, evaluating **Promoter Quality** is the first line of defence.

## Lesson 20.1 — The Two-Tier Ownership Architecture: Sticky Promoters vs. Dispersed Float

```
                               NEPSE TWO-TIER EQUITY STRUCTURE
   ┌───────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Promoter Shares (P / PO Symbol)   │ Ordinary Public Shares            │
   ├───────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
   │ • Minimum 51% floor for BFIs.     │ • Freely tradable on NEPSE.       │
   │ • 3-year statutory lock-in (hydro)│ • Liquid continuous order book.   │
   │   or 5-year lock-in (BAFIA).      │ • Dispersed retail holdings.      │
   │ • Transfers require NRB approval. │ • Priced at market supply/demand. │
   │ • 30%–50% historical OTC discount.│ • Standard par value Rs. 100.     │
   └───────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Why Promoter Dominance Persists
1. **Mandated Control:** NRB regulations enforce a minimum 51% promoter holding in commercial banks.
2. **Permanent Board Plurality:** Minority retail investors rarely coordinate voting at AGMs, allowing promoter families to control board appointments perpetually.

## Lesson 20.2 — Incentive Misalignment: Where Promoter Economics Diverge

```
                            THE THREE CHANNELS OF VALUE DIVERGENCE
   ┌───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┐
   │ 1. Related-Party Rent │ 2. Share Pledging     │ 3. The At-Par Rights  │
   │    Extraction         │    Liquidation Risk   │    Issue Squeeze      │
   │ Directing procurement │ Borrowing against     │ Issuing rights at par │
   │ to private entities at│ shares for personal   │ (Rs. 100) when shares │
   │ above-market rates.   │ debt creates margin   │ trade at Rs. 500+,    │
   │                       │ call forced selling.  │ absorbing devolvement.│
   └───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┘
```

> **The At-Par Rights Issue Trap:** When a company trading at Rs. 600 issues a 1:1 rights issue at Rs. 100 par value, well-capitalized promoters subscribe in full and absorb devolved (unsubscribed) shares via auction, permanently increasing their equity proportion while cash-constrained retail holders suffer massive dilution.

## Lesson 20.3 — The Promoter Playbook: Tactics to Watch For

1. **Unbenchmarked Related-Party Dealings:** Routing insurance brokerage, IT consulting, real estate leasing, and construction capex through promoter-affiliated subsidiaries without competitive tenders.
2. **Boardroom Capture & Feuds:** When promoter factions fight over management control, corporate stability collapses.
   * *Historical Case:* In **2014, Nepal Credit and Commerce Bank (NCC Bank)** suffered a complete board paralysis lasting 3 months due to a 51% promoter faction feud, compelling Nepal Rastra Bank to seize direct management control.
3. **Information Asymmetry:** Insiders receive weekly operating metrics (NPL trends, loan disbursements) months before quarterly NFRS disclosures reach the public.

## Lesson 20.4 — Reading Promoter Shareholding Trends

```
                              PROMOTER ACTION SIGNAL MATRIX
   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Observable Promoter Action                    │ Underlying Economic Signal        │
   ├───────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
   │ Open-market buying / full rights take-up      │ Strong conviction and alignment.  │
   │ Stake dilution strictly via fresh IPO/FPO     │ Neutral mechanical dilution.      │
   │ Active OTC sell-down outside lock-in expiry   │ Loss of conviction / cash strain. │
   │ Heavy selling immediately at lock-in expiry   │ Anticipate temporary price drop.  │
   │ Rising share pledge filings                   │ High leverage & forced-sale risk. │
   └───────────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┘
```

*SEBON Mandate:* Listed companies must publish a formal public notice **30 days prior to promoter lock-in expiration**, providing a clear calendar window for risk rebalancing.

## Lesson 20.6 — The 8-Point Practical Promoter Quality Scorecard

Before purchasing any NEPSE scrip, evaluate the promoter group against this **8-Dimension Scorecard**:

| # | Dimension | Favourable Signal (Green) | Unfavourable Signal (Red) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Shareholding Trend** | Stable or increasing via open market / rights. | Active OTC sell-down without clear benign cause. |
| **2** | **Share Pledging** | Zero or minimal shares pledged for debt. | Substantial, undisclosed, or rising share pledges. |
| **3** | **Rights Issue History** | Fair capital calls; transparent allocation. | Repeated at-par rights issues with devolvement absorption. |
| **4** | **Board Independence** | Credible independent directors with finance expertise. | Family-dominated board with no independent check. |
| **5** | **Related-Party Transparency**| Explicit counterparty naming & market benchmarking. | Vague, unquantified related-party footnotes. |
| **6** | **Dividend Consistency** | Regular cash payouts aligned with cash flow. | Eradicated dividends coinciding with promoter cash needs. |
| **7** | **Regulatory Track Record**| Zero NRB/SEBON penalties or board takeovers. | History of central bank prompt corrective actions (PCA). |
| **8** | **Research & Disclosure** | Transparent investor disclosures; prompt filings. | Bare minimum statutory disclosures; chronic delays. |

### Chapter Summary & Strategic Takeaways

- [ ] **Sticky Control is Permanent:** In banking, the 51% promoter floor makes minority shareholders permanent junior partners.
- [ ] **Scrutinize Share Pledging:** Margin calls on promoter personal debt create unexpected supply gluts.
- [ ] **Calendar Lock-In Expiry Dates:** Track mandatory 30-day SEBON lock-in expiration notices.
- [ ] **Score Promoters Systematically:** Apply the 8-point scorecard at every AGM and quarterly disclosure cycle.
