When a company asks existing shareholders for more money, the question is never simply whether to give it — it is whether the terms are fair, the capital will be used wisely, and you are being treated as an owner or a source of funds.

Lesson 15.1 — Why Rights Issues Dominate Capital Raising in Nepal

Underdeveloped corporate bond markets, regulatory minimum capital directives (NRB's Rs. 8 Arba commercial bank mandate), and promoter control retention make Rights Issues the primary equity expansion tool on NEPSE. Shareholders who take no action suffer direct uncompensated dilution.

Lesson 15.2 — Rights Ratio, Rights Price, and TERP

The Theoretical Ex-Rights Price (TERP) calculates the rational post-rights baseline clearing price:

TERP = [ (N × Cum-Rights Price) + (R × Rights Price) ] / (N + R)

For a 1:2 issue at Rs. 100 with a pre-rights price of Rs. 400, TERP is Rs. 300, and the intrinsic value of the right is Rs. 100 per existing share.

Lesson 15.3 — Subscribe, Renounce, or Sell

Scenario Recommended Action Underlying Rational Logic
High ROE / Productive Capex Subscribe in Full Incremental equity creates compounding returns above cost of capital.
Stagnant ROE / High TERP Renounce / Sell Rights Avoid pumping cash into low-yielding capital bases.
Cash Constrained Partial Subscribe & Sell Balance Monetize unexercised rights entitlement to avoid pure dilution loss.
Unsubscribed Rights Sealed-Bid Public Auction Remaining unsubscribed shares are auctioned via competitive bidding.

Lesson 15.4 — FPOs in Nepal

Governed by the Securities Registration and Issuance Regulation 2073, Further Public Offerings (FPOs) invite the general public to subscribe to new equity at premium prices, broadening public float and reducing excess promoter concentrations.

Lesson 15.5 — The Equity Dilution Problem

When paid-up capital compounds at 20% while net earnings grow at 10%, Return on Equity (ROE) collapses. Dilution without proportionate profit expansion turns book value growth into a wealth-destroying trap for minority shareholders.
